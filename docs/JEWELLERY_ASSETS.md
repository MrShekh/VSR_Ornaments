# Jewellery preview assets

Created using the built-in image-generation tool. These are illustrative product and on-model previews, not verified VSR inventory photography. Prices and specifications in `data/products.ts` are demonstration content pending client approval.

Each `public/images/products/{arc,lumen,trace,line,axis}-{white,gold,rose}-gallery.png` contains two equal square panels: product on the left, worn on the right. The product image component selects a panel through CSS; the original files remain intact. Metal switching changes the asset, not a global colour filter. All fifteen files were visually inspected.

Replace the paired gallery assets with approved product photography and confirm metal availability, specifications, prices and arrival flags before publication. Wishlist is browser-local, not account-synced.
